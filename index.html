Dynamic Magneto-Mechanical Coupled Inversion in HTS Solenoids

Constitutive Relations: Maxwell-Lorentz Mechanics Coupled to Elastic Ring Dilatation

In Superconducting Magnetic Energy Storage (SMES) coils wound with second-generation rare-earth barium copper oxide (2G HTS REBCO) tapes, current densities reach hundreds of amperes per square millimeter. The cross product of winding current $\mathbf{J}$ and self-generated magnetic flux density $\mathbf{B}$ imposes outward radial body forces. Under elastic hoop stress, the solenoid expands uniformly. However, tape delamination causes immediate radial gap formation, discontinuous increase in cross-sectional enclosed magnetic flux area $A$, and a signature step change in incremental self-inductance $\frac{\partial L}{\partial F}$.

1

Lorentz Body Force & Hoop Stress Distribution

The local body force density $\mathbf{f}_L$ inside the tape packs is governed by the Lorentz relation. In cylindrical coordinates $(r, \theta, z)$, with azimuthal transport current $J_\theta$ and axial magnetic flux $B_z$:

$$\mathbf{f}_L = \mathbf{J} \times \mathbf{B} = J_\theta B_z(r,z) \,\hat{\mathbf{r}} - J_\theta B_r(r,z) \,\hat{\mathbf{z}}$$

Integrating over the active coil volume $V_c$ yields the integrated radial outward hoop force $F_r$, producing circumferential hoop tension $\sigma_\theta$:

$$\sigma_\theta(r) = \frac{F_r}{2\pi t_w h_w} = \frac{1}{t_w h_w} \int_0^h \int_{R_{\text{in}}}^{R_{\text{out}}} J_\theta(r) B_z(r,z) r \, dr \, dz$$
Critical REBCO Limit: Delamination occurs when radial peel stress $\sigma_r \ge 25 \text{ MPa}$ or transverse tension $\sigma_c \ge 400 \text{ MPa}$.
2

Geometric Self-Inductance Dilatation Coupling

The nominal self-inductance $L_0$ of a multi-turn solenoid with radius $R$, length $l_c$, turn count $N$, and Nagaoka coefficient $K_N$ satisfies:

$$L_0 = \mu_0 N^2 \frac{\pi R^2}{l_c} K_N\left(\frac{2R}{l_c}\right)$$

Under elastic strain $\varepsilon_\theta = \frac{\Delta R}{R} = \frac{\sigma_\theta}{E_{\text{composite}}}$, the radius dilates. Taking the first derivative with respect to Lorentz radial force $F_r$:

$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial F_r} = L_0 \left[ \frac{2}{R}\frac{\partial R}{\partial F_r} - \frac{1}{l_c}\frac{\partial l_c}{\partial F_r} \right] \approx \frac{2 L_0}{2\pi R \cdot (E A)_{\text{equiv}}}$$
Baseline Elasticity: Linear, predictable slope $\frac{\partial L}{\partial F}$ dictated purely by Young's modulus of Hastelloy C-276 substrate.
3

State Observer Inversion & Delamination Discontinuity

Terminal terminal voltage $V(t)$, current $I(t)$, and shunt resistance $R_s$ provide real-time dynamic inductance tracking without invasive interior optical fibers:

$$L_{\text{meas}}(t) = \frac{V(t) - I(t) R_{\text{joint}} - \sum M_{ij}\frac{dI_j}{dt}}{\frac{dI}{dt}}$$

When inter-layer epoxy or solder matrix shears (delamination), the outer tape turns snap outward by gap distance $\delta_{\text{sep}}$, inducing an instantaneous jump:

$$\Delta L_{\text{delam}} = \mu_0 N_{\text{outer}}^2 \frac{\pi \left[(R + \delta_{\text{sep}})^2 - R^2\right]}{l_c} > \xi_{\text{threshold}}$$
Sentinel Criterion: If $\frac{d^2 L}{d F^2} \ge \Gamma_{\text{crit}}$, trigger gate-turn-off thyristors within 8 milliseconds.
